# 🇭🇹 Haitian Creole

Haitian Creole is an important part of my communication foundation and cultural identity.

This section focuses on strengthening my ability to communicate clearly and effectively in Haitian Creole across different situations.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop strong Haitian Creole communication skills through reading, writing, speaking, listening, and technical vocabulary.

---

## 📚 Focus

### 📖 Reading

- Improve reading comprehension
- Understand different types of texts
- Expand vocabulary
- Identify main ideas and supporting information

### ✍️ Writing

- Write clearly and correctly
- Improve grammar and sentence structure
- Organize ideas effectively
- Develop different forms of writing

### 🗣️ Speaking

- Communicate ideas clearly
- Improve pronunciation and fluency
- Develop confidence in conversations
- Explain technical and complex ideas

### 👂 Listening

- Improve comprehension
- Understand different speaking styles
- Identify key information
- Follow conversations and explanations

### 💻 Technical Vocabulary

- Learn technology and software terminology
- Understand technical concepts in Haitian Creole
- Develop the ability to explain technical ideas clearly
- Build vocabulary related to Software Engineering and other fields

---

## 🏆 Goal

Develop strong and practical Haitian Creole communication skills that support learning, collaboration, everyday communication, and the ability to explain complex ideas clearly.

# 🇫🇷 French

French is an important language for communication, education, access to knowledge, and international collaboration.

This section focuses on developing practical French communication skills that can support both everyday life and professional development.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop strong French communication skills through reading, writing, speaking, listening, and technical vocabulary.

---

## 📚 Focus

### 📖 Reading

- Improve reading comprehension
- Understand different types of texts
- Expand vocabulary
- Identify main ideas and supporting information

### ✍️ Writing

- Write clearly and correctly
- Improve grammar and sentence structure
- Organize ideas effectively
- Develop different forms of writing

### 🗣️ Speaking

- Communicate ideas clearly
- Improve pronunciation and fluency
- Develop confidence in conversations
- Explain technical and complex ideas

### 👂 Listening

- Improve listening comprehension
- Understand different speaking styles
- Identify key information
- Follow conversations and explanations

### 💻 Technical Vocabulary

- Learn technology and software terminology
- Understand technical concepts in French
- Explain technical ideas clearly
- Build vocabulary related to Software Engineering and other fields

---

## 🏆 Goal

Develop strong and practical French communication skills that support learning, collaboration, professional communication, and international opportunities.

# 🇪🇸 Spanish

Spanish is an important international language that expands communication, cultural understanding, and access to people and knowledge across different regions of the world.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop strong Spanish communication skills through reading, writing, speaking, listening, and technical vocabulary.

---

## 📚 Focus

### 📖 Reading
- Improve reading comprehension
- Understand different types of texts
- Expand vocabulary
- Identify main ideas and supporting information

### ✍️ Writing
- Write clearly and correctly
- Improve grammar and sentence structure
- Organize ideas effectively
- Develop practical writing skills

### 🗣️ Speaking
- Communicate ideas clearly
- Improve pronunciation and fluency
- Build confidence in conversations
- Explain ideas effectively

### 👂 Listening
- Improve listening comprehension
- Understand different speaking styles
- Follow conversations and explanations
- Identify key information

### 💻 Technical Vocabulary
- Learn technology terminology
- Understand technical concepts in Spanish
- Build software engineering vocabulary
- Explain technical ideas clearly

---

## 🏆 Goal

Develop practical Spanish communication skills that support learning, communication, cultural understanding, and future international opportunities.

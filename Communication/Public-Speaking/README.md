# 🎤 Public Speaking

Public speaking is the ability to communicate ideas clearly and confidently to an audience.

For an engineer, strong public speaking skills make it easier to explain technical concepts, present projects, share knowledge, and communicate with different audiences.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop the ability to speak clearly, confidently, and effectively in front of individuals and groups.

---

## 📚 Topics

- Speech Structure
- Clarity
- Voice and Pronunciation
- Body Language
- Audience Awareness
- Confidence
- Explaining Ideas
- Handling Questions
- Impromptu Speaking
- Speaking Practice

---

## 🏆 Goal

Become a clear and confident speaker who can explain ideas, present projects, and communicate effectively with different audiences.

# 👂 Active Listening

Active listening is the ability to fully understand what another person is communicating before responding.

It is essential for engineers because understanding users, teammates, requirements, and feedback leads to better decisions and better software.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop the ability to listen carefully, understand information, ask useful questions, and respond thoughtfully.

---

## 📚 Topics

- Attention
- Understanding
- Asking Questions
- Clarification
- Paraphrasing
- Feedback
- Empathy
- Identifying Key Information
- Avoiding Assumptions
- Listening Practice

---

## 🏆 Goal

Become an attentive and thoughtful listener who can understand people, requirements, feedback, and ideas accurately.

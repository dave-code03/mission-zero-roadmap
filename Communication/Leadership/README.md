# 🧭 Leadership

Leadership is the ability to guide, support, and influence people toward a shared goal.

For an engineer, leadership includes taking responsibility, helping teams work effectively, making thoughtful decisions, and supporting others.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop the communication, responsibility, and collaboration skills required to lead effectively.

---

## 📚 Topics

- Responsibility
- Decision Making
- Communication
- Team Guidance
- Delegation
- Problem Solving
- Accountability
- Feedback
- Conflict Resolution
- Servant Leadership
- Continuous Improvement

---

## 🏆 Goal

Develop the ability to lead responsibly, support teammates, make effective decisions, and contribute positively to successful projects.

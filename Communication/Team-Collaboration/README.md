# 🤝 Team Collaboration

Team collaboration is the ability to work effectively with others toward a shared objective.

Software engineering is often a team activity, making communication, cooperation, responsibility, and trust essential skills.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop the skills required to communicate, cooperate, contribute, and solve problems effectively within a team.

---

## 📚 Topics

- Team Communication
- Cooperation
- Roles and Responsibilities
- Knowledge Sharing
- Feedback
- Conflict Resolution
- Problem Solving
- Respect
- Accountability
- Working Toward Shared Goals

---

## 🏆 Goal

Become an effective team member who communicates clearly, contributes responsibly, supports others, and helps teams achieve shared goals.

# 💪 Confidence

Confidence is the ability to trust your knowledge, skills, judgment, and ability to communicate effectively.

For an engineer, confidence helps with learning, presenting ideas, asking questions, accepting feedback, and working with others.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop healthy confidence that supports effective communication, learning, decision making, and professional growth.

---

## 📚 Topics

- Self-Awareness
- Self-Trust
- Speaking with Confidence
- Asking Questions
- Expressing Ideas
- Handling Mistakes
- Receiving Feedback
- Managing Fear
- Decision Making
- Confidence Through Practice

---

## 🏆 Goal

Build the confidence needed to communicate ideas clearly, participate actively, learn from mistakes, and contribute effectively to teams and projects.

# ⚛️ Physics

Physics helps us understand how the universe works through observation, experimentation, and mathematical laws.

It explains motion, energy, matter, forces, electricity, light, and the fundamental principles that govern nature.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop scientific thinking and understand the physical laws that support engineering, technology, and artificial intelligence.

---

## 📚 Topics

- Mechanics
- Motion
- Forces
- Energy
- Work & Power
- Electricity
- Magnetism
- Waves
- Light (Optics)
- Thermodynamics
- Modern Physics

---

## 🏆 Goal

Build a solid understanding of physics as a foundation for engineering, robotics, electronics, and advanced technologies.

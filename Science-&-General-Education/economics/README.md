# 💹 Economics

Economics is the study of how people, businesses, and governments make decisions about resources, production, and wealth.

Understanding economics helps explain markets, innovation, entrepreneurship, and how technology creates value.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop economic thinking to understand value creation, financial systems, entrepreneurship, and sustainable growth.

---

## 📚 Topics

- Microeconomics
- Macroeconomics
- Supply & Demand
- Markets
- Money & Banking
- Inflation
- International Trade
- Personal Finance
- Entrepreneurship
- Digital Economy

---

## 🏆 Goal

Build economic knowledge to make better financial decisions, understand global markets, and create products that solve real-world problems.

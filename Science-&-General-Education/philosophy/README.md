# 🧠 Philosophy

Philosophy is the study of knowledge, reasoning, ethics, reality, and critical thinking.

It helps us ask better questions, think clearly, make rational decisions, and understand the principles behind science, technology, and human behavior.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop logical reasoning, ethical thinking, and intellectual curiosity to become a better engineer, leader, and lifelong learner.

---

## 📚 Topics

- Logic
- Critical Thinking
- Ethics
- Epistemology
- Philosophy of Science
- Philosophy of Technology
- Problem Solving
- Decision Making

---

## 🏆 Goal

Build a strong mindset for reasoning, innovation, responsible technology, and continuous learning.

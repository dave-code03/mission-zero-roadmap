# 📜 History

History is the study of past events, civilizations, cultures, and the evolution of humanity.

Understanding history helps us learn from past successes and mistakes, recognize patterns, and make better decisions for the future.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop historical awareness and understand how past events have shaped today's world.

- Middle Ages
- Modern History
- World Wars
- Scientific Revolution
- Industrial Revolution
- Digital Revolution
- History of Computing
- Haitian History
- World History

---

## 🏆 Goal

Build historical knowledge to better understand society, innovation, leadership, and the evolution of technology.

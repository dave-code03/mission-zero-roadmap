# 🌍 Geography

Geography is the study of the Earth, its environments, people, and the relationships between them.

It helps us understand the world, different cultures, natural resources, climate, and how geography influences human life and technology.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop a global understanding of our planet and how geography shapes societies, economies, and technological development.

---

## 📚 Topics

- Physical Geography
- Human Geography
- Climate
- Ecosystems
- Natural Resources
- Maps & Navigation
- Continents & Oceans
- Countries & Cultures
- Population
- Environmental Challenges

---

## 🏆 Goal

Build geographical knowledge to better understand the world, global challenges, and international collaboration.

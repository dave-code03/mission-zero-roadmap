# 📐 Mathematics

Mathematics is the language of logic, patterns, and problem solving.

It provides the foundation for Computer Science, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, and many other fields.

## Mission

Build strong mathematical thinking to solve problems, understand algorithms, and develop intelligent systems.

## Topics

- Arithmetic
- Algebra
- Geometry
- Trigonometry
- Calculus
- Linear Algebra
- Probability
- Statistics
- Discrete Mathematics

## Goal

Develop mathematical reasoning as a foundation for engineering and artificial intelligence.

# 🧠 Critical Thinking

Critical thinking is the ability to analyze information, evaluate evidence, question assumptions, and form reasoned conclusions.

It helps distinguish reliable information from weak reasoning and supports better decisions and problem solving.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop the ability to think clearly, question assumptions, evaluate information, and make decisions based on evidence and reasoning.

---

## 📚 Topics

- Observation
- Asking Questions
- Assumptions
- Evidence
- Facts vs Opinions
- Logical Reasoning
- Arguments
- Bias
- Evaluating Sources
- Identifying Fallacies
- Comparing Perspectives
- Drawing Conclusions
- Decision Making

---

## 🛠️ Goal

Develop strong reasoning skills that can be applied to learning, problem solving, decision making, and everyday situations.

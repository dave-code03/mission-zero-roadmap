# 💪 Discipline

Discipline is the ability to consistently take the actions required to reach a goal, even when motivation is low.

It provides the consistency needed to build strong habits, maintain progress, and complete long-term goals.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop the discipline required to learn consistently, complete planned work, and maintain progress over the long term.

---

## 📚 Topics

- Consistency
- Habits
- Time Commitment
- Focus
- Self-Control
- Responsibility
- Following Plans
- Overcoming Procrastination
- Long-Term Thinking

---

## 🛠️ Goal

Build reliable habits and consistent work patterns that support continuous learning, personal growth, and professional development.

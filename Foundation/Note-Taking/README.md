# 📝 Note Taking

Note taking is the practice of capturing, organizing, processing, and reviewing information in a way that makes knowledge easier to understand and remember.

Effective notes should help transform information into useful knowledge rather than simply copying information.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop a practical note-taking system that supports understanding, retention, organization, and future retrieval of knowledge.

---

## 📚 Topics

- Purpose of Note Taking
- Active Note Taking
- Organizing Information
- Summarizing
- Key Ideas
- Questions and Connections
- Structured Notes
- Digital Notes
- Reviewing Notes
- Knowledge Organization
- Building a Personal Knowledge Base

---

## 🛠️ Goal

Create clear, useful, and organized notes that support learning, problem solving, and long-term knowledge development.

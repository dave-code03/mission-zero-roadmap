# ⚡ Productivity

Productivity is the ability to use time, energy, and resources effectively to make meaningful progress toward important goals.

Good productivity is not about doing more work. It is about focusing on the right work and completing it effectively.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Develop a practical productivity system for organizing work, managing time, maintaining focus, and consistently making progress.

---

## 📚 Topics

- Goal Setting
- Prioritization
- Time Management
- Task Management
- Planning
- Focus
- Work Sessions
- Managing Distractions
- Progress Tracking
- Continuous Improvement

---

## 🛠️ Goal

Build effective productivity habits that make it possible to organize responsibilities, focus on important work, and consistently move toward long-term goals.
